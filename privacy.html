Privacy Policy

Coudraie Films IA - last updated October 2, 2026

This policy explains what data Coudraie Films IA ("the Service") handles when it connects to TikTok.

1. Data we access

  • Basic profile info from TikTok (open id, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope, only to show which account is connected.
  • Upload permission via the video.upload scope, only to send our own videos to that account.

2. What we do not do

We do not read your messages, followers, or private videos. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted by our integration provider (Composio) and used only to perform uploads requested by the account owner. No TikTok data is kept beyond what is needed for this purpose.

4. Retention and deletion

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions); tokens then stop working. To request deletion of any stored data, message us on Telegram and it will be removed within 30 days.

5. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
